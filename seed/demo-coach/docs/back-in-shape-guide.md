# Getting back in shape after a break: Alex's guide

## The first two weeks
- 2-3 workouts a week, 40 minutes each
- Light weights: stop while 3-4 reps are still "in reserve"
- The goal: learn the movements and build a habit, not break records
- Muscle soreness in the first couple of days is normal. Sharp or joint pain: stop and report it

## Weeks 3-6
- Move up to RPE 7-8
- Double progression: reach the top of the rep range on every set, then add weight
- Log every workout (weight, sets and reps); that's what lets you progress

## Week 7: deload
- The same exercises, about 60% of the sets, slightly lighter weights
- A recovery week that prepares you for the next block

## Habits that hold it all together
- Put workouts in your calendar like a meeting
- Steps: start at 6,000 and add 1,000 each week up to 10,000
- Sleep: the same wake-up time every day, weekends too
- Not perfect, consistent: a week with one workout beats a week with nothing

## When to stop and see a doctor
- Chest pain, unusual shortness of breath, dizziness or fainting: stop immediately
- Pain radiating to a leg or an arm, numbness or weakness
- Joint pain that doesn't fade within 48 hours or gets worse
