# Sample meal plan with the hand method (a normal day)

This plan suits most trainees. Adjust amounts by your hand size and your goal. No food is forbidden; there are portions.

## Breakfast
- 2-3 eggs (or shakshuka) or protein yogurt with granola
- 1-2 slices of whole-grain bread
- Cut vegetables: tomato, cucumber, bell pepper
- A spoon of tahini or a quarter of an avocado

## Lunch
- Chicken breast / fish / turkey meatballs / tofu: one or two palms
- Rice, sweet potato or couscous: one or two cupped hands
- A big salad or cooked vegetables: half the plate
- A spoon of olive oil on the salad

## Snack
- Fruit + a handful of nuts
- Or: cottage cheese with whole-grain crackers
- Or: a protein shake with a banana (on busy days)

## Dinner
- Tuna / eggs / cottage cheese / legumes: one or two palms
- Vegetables or vegetable soup
- Carbs: one portion (or half a portion during fat loss)

## Tips
- Prep protein for 2-3 days ahead (oven chicken, hard-boiled eggs, cooked legumes)
- Drink a glass of water before each meal
- Eating out: pick a dish with a clear protein source, add a salad, and enjoy it without guilt
